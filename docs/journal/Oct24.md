First, we ran a sweep test from GMM2 to GMM5 over 2004–2013 and found that, with PCA, GMM2, GMM4, and GMM5 were not competitive against GMM3 ([gmm_sweep.ipynb](notebooks/sp500/gmm_sweep.ipynb)). Then, we created plots to inspect the fitted components and saw that GMM2 and GMM3 captured the main differences between the bear and bull distributions, while GMM4 and GMM5 seemed to use one or two components to fit the long tails, suggesting possible overfitting ([gmm_distribution.ipynb](notebooks/sp500/gmm_distribution.ipynb)).
